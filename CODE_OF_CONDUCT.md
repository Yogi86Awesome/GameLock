# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
GameLock a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to PowerShell or to Windows
  internals.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who rely on it.
- Remembering that many users of this tool are trying to change a habit, and
  treating that with respect.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, harassment, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Mocking people for the reasons they use a playtime limiter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
will take appropriate, fair action in response to behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. That may include editing or
removing comments, commits, issues, and pull requests, or temporarily or
permanently banning a contributor.

Report problems by opening an issue marked "conduct", or, if the matter is
private, through a GitHub private vulnerability report on this repository.
All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy will be respected.
